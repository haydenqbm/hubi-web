# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors exploring Hubi products with the intent to contact Hubi and buy.

## Product Purpose

A Vietnamese public website that presents Hubi products, helps visitors browse the range, and leads qualified visitors to contact Hubi to buy.

## Positioning

The site is a calm, sophisticated product showcase with a clear browse-to-contact journey.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Routes include the homepage, product listing and detail pages, blog listing and detail pages, and contact.
- V1 is a static public showcase without checkout, authentication, CMS, or backend integration.
- Product proof strategy and supporting content remain open for the user to define during design.
- Reusable prebuilt UI and animation components from 21st.dev and React Bits are preferred and must be adapted into one coherent system.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Hubi Việt Nam.
- The visual tone should feel calm and sophisticated.
- Major display typography: Noto Serif, weights 500–600.
- Major body/interface typography: Noto Sans, weights 400–500.
- Core palette supplied by the user: #F1E8D9, #F7F1E8, #E0CCB3, #07676E, #1A585F, #698E93, #B5C7CA, #2A2C2D, #5D6555, #A6A7A1.
- Neutral derivatives and accessibility-safe variants may be derived from the supplied palette.

## Product Principles

- Make browsing feel composed and easy.
- Lead visitors naturally from products to contact.
- Keep the visual system calm, sophisticated, and consistent across the site.
- Prefer focused, purposeful motion over spectacle.
- Preserve room for product-specific proof to be added deliberately.
